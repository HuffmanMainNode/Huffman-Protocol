# Protocol or interoperability proposal

## Contribution type

Choose one: new specification, implementation, adapter, conformance vector, evaluation, or documentation.

## Problem statement

Describe the concrete interoperability or verification problem. Identify affected systems and users.

## Existing work review

List relevant standards, drafts, repositories, and implementations. Explain what they already solve and what remains unresolved. Do not claim novelty without this comparison.

## Proposed contribution

Describe the smallest testable contribution. Identify whether it is original work, an adaptation, or an integration.

## Compatibility

Identify versions, interfaces, and expected interactions with existing protocols.

## Evidence and acceptance criteria

Define observable success, reproducible test cases, negative cases, and independent verification requirements.

## Security and privacy

Describe trust boundaries, execution requirements, abuse cases, data exposure, and mitigations. Never include credentials or private operational details.

## Licensing and provenance

Identify authorship, source materials, dependencies, licenses, and required attribution.

## Limitations and open questions

List what the proposal does not solve and what remains unverified.

## AI-assisted submission

If an AI system helped prepare this proposal, identify the assistance and the human or organization responsible for review and submission.
