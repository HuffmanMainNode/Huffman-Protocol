# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free environment for everyone.

Expected behavior includes respectful technical criticism, good-faith questions, credit and attribution, disclosure of conflicts, and respect for privacy and security boundaries.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, intimidation, deliberate disruption, and unauthorized publication of private information are unacceptable.

Report concerns privately to the maintainers. Reports will be reviewed fairly and handled with appropriate confidentiality. Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation to protect the community.
